HOLDOUT MODEL EVALUATION

Evaluate a dataset

Upload an approved, de-identified CSV to measure a simple model on held-out rows. CSV rows are evaluated in memory; summary metrics and fitted coefficients are retained. The latest mentor-trained model is shared for score estimates across portal accounts.

Privacy first: do not upload names, emails, IDs, or sensitive information. This local build has no data-governance review; use synthetic or approved de-identified research data only. The random row split is not student-grouped and should not support research claims for repeated student records.
